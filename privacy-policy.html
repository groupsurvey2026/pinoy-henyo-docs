Privacy Policy

Pinoy Henyo  ·  Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how John Martin Tambaoan ("I", "me", or "my") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use Pinoy Henyo ("the App").

1. Information I Collect

When you use Pinoy Henyo, I collect the following information:

  • Google account information: your name, email address, and profile photo, collected when you sign in with Google
  • Display name: the game handle you choose during onboarding
  • Game data: your quiz answers, scores, accuracy rates, and leaderboard rankings
  • Device information: basic device and operating system information collected automatically by the app

2. How I Use Your Information

I use the information collected to:

  • Create and manage your game account
  • Display your scores and rankings on leaderboards
  • Track your game progress across sessions
  • Improve the App's performance and content

3. Information Sharing

I do not sell your personal information. Your display name and leaderboard scores are visible to other players within the App. I do not share your personal information with third parties except as required by law.

The App uses Supabase for data storage and Google OAuth for authentication. These services have their own privacy policies governing their use of your data.

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting me at kurt.rull@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

Pinoy Henyo is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. I will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
